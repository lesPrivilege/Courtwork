FE-05 · Material / blur specimen

Round 6/6. One question: on Courtwork's already-stable geometry, typography and interaction hierarchy, which short functional surfaces gain useful layer separation from translucency, and which should stay solid.

Material is the final dependent variable. Composition, typography, shape, interaction semantics and placement are all held constant. Blur radii are fixed at the two existing tokens — --blur-chrome 12px and --blur-transient 16px — and no third radius is compared.

Base: see README.md. FE-05a has not landed on main; this specimen consumes actual main, not a private imitation of a future baseline.

  if (!recorded.length) return { state: "unknown", rows: [] };
  return {
    state: run.outcome ?? "unknown",
    rows: recorded.map(toRow),
