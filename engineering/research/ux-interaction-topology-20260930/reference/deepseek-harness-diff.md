## 3. Where DeepSeek harness contradicts or pressures one of our rulings

1. **UX-11 / S2 flat list.** DS uses root-menu-then-drill panes with provider group headings (`ModelSelect.tsx:45-46,505`). Our S2 requires a flat single-disclosure list with provider as trailing metadata. Their own decision note prefers the grouped menu. Reviewer view: keep S2; DS is at depth 3 actions where we target 2.
2. **UX-11 scope wording.** DS writes the global default on every per-session pick (`commands.ts:147-173`) and states it only in docs (`providers.md:37`), not on the control. That violates our "a working-surface control that writes a global default says so" rule; not a model to copy.
3. **Ruling J (unkeyed models listed but blocked).** DS lets a stale selection fail at Send (`ui-model-selection/README.md:50`) and hides unconfigured providers. Ours is stricter by design.
4. **"No queue" (role-composer README:29,69) and S1 CMP-14.** DS ships Queue and Steer with a setting and QueueDock; it depends on a Host steering operation.
5. **Stop has no shortcut (`settings-view.mjs:2880`).** DS binds Esc Esc; its double-press answers our accidental-stop objection.
6. **Agent choice frozen at first message (DS) vs changeable per chat (CMP-03).** Different product model; DS does not contradict the ruling, but shows the opposite trade.
7. **Header rename (S6, HDR-01).** No DS precedent: they rename from the sidebar row and by double-click, leaving the header title inert.
