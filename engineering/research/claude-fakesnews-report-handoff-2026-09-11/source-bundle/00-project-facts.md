# 00 · Project facts

These are the bounded project facts available to the writer. They are not promotional claims and do not close the project's open product gates.

## Courtwork

- The frozen early project position describes Courtwork as a local-first Work Agent GUI rather than a chatbot.
- Its stated separation is model generation and summarisation; deterministic checking of coordinates, scope, authorization, contracts, budgets, and irreversible actions; and human confirmation/finalization.
- Current architecture assigns Matter, Candidate, Decision, Artifact, sources, and formal commitment state to Work Core.
- Host, Runtime, GUI, and adapters may project or execute facts but are not a second accepted-work authority.
- Provider and Runtime are replacement axes; changing a model should not silently change completion semantics.
- A successful tool call, completed Run, or visible UI control does not itself grant acceptance authority.
- Synthetic fixtures, screenshots, local checks, and published pages each have limited evidence scope.
