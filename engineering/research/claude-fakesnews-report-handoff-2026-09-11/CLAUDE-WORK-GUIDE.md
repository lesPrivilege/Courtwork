# Claude work guide · fictional investigative report

## Role

You are a writer producing a clearly fictional, satirical technology report from the supplied offline dossier. The report should feel observant and institutionally plausible, but its fiction status must be explicit in the final package. Treat the material as four separate lanes: **Observed**, **Reported claim**, **Interpretation**, and **Satire**.

## Core assignment

Write a 1,200–2,000 word report about the fictional lab/vendor `les Privilege`, its work system Courtwork, and the Schema Engineering Paper. The report should investigate the mismatch between a small or fictive institution and the gravity of frontier-lab language: durable work, models as replaceable runtimes, evidence, authority, provenance, and human acceptance.

The report may use these fictional names as supplied creative material:

- `les Privilege`
- `莱斯·普里维莱吉` as a conventional fictional Chinese media rendering
- `莱索·普里维克` as an intentionally unfamiliar fictional rendering
- `蕾丝·普里维莱吉` as a literary/brand pun, not the sole canonical name
- `Juliana Sorel / 朱莉安娜·索蕾尔` as a fictional founder character

Do not present any of those as a real company, real person, legal entity, or verified translation.

## Suggested shape

1. Headline and deck that initially read like a serious technology report.
2. Lede: a lab with an unusually serious work contract but no claimed foundation model.
3. Courtwork and Schema Engineering: Matter, Candidate, Decision, Artifact, Context, Run, evidence, review, and human acceptance.
4. The brand and founder as a parallel-world construction: `les`, `蕾丝`, `Lovelace`, `Juliana Sorel`, and the delayed recognition of literary echoes.
5. Open/closed knowledge flow and provider observation privilege as questions, not verdicts.
6. A closing reversal that reveals the report's institutional gravity as the object of satire.
7. An explicit editor's note: fictional reporting/satire; real-company references are public-context comparisons only.

This shape is a suggestion, not a command to invent facts. A shorter or different structure is acceptable if it preserves evidence discipline.
