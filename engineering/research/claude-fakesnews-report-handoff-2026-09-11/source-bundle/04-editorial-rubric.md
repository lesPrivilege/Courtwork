# 04 · Editorial rubric

## Required labels in the companion ledger

- **Observed:** directly present in the supplied fixed project materials.
- **Reported claim:** a public organization's own statement, attributed to that organization.
- **Interpretation:** a reasoned connection or semiotic reading.
- **Satire:** an intentionally fictional exaggeration aimed at the invented lab or its institutional voice.
- **Open question:** plausible but not closed by the package.

## Quality gates

1. The fiction disclosure is visible in `report.md` and `README.md`.
2. Real organizations are discussed only through attributed public context.
3. No real-person anonymous quote, invented employee, invented customer, or invented legal fact appears.
4. The report distinguishes project facts from the fictional founder and fictional lab.
5. The open/closed knowledge-flow argument is framed as a question, not a proven causal timeline.
6. The report does not turn UI, brand geometry, screenshots, or author checks into product acceptance.
7. The prose may be serious, but it does not impersonate a named publication or claim publication by a real outlet.

## Suggested final disclosure

Use a clear editor's note in substance: this is fictional satire; `les Privilege` and its founder are invented; real-company references are public-context comparisons and are not allegations or independent findings.
