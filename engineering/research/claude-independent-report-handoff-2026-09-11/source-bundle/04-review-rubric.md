# 04 · Review rubric

The companion ledger must distinguish:

- **Observed:** directly present in fixed project material;
- **Reported claim:** a public organization's own statement, explicitly attributed;
- **Interpretation:** a reasoned connection across supplied material;
- **Open question:** unresolved or requiring evidence outside this package.

Quality gates:

1. No invented people, customers, employees, funding, legal status, anonymous quotes, or interviews.
2. Real organizations appear only through attributed public context.
3. Project facts remain separate from naming and literary associations.
4. The open/closed knowledge-flow discussion stays a question, not a proven causal timeline.
5. Screenshots, pages, fixtures, author checks, and brand geometry do not become proof of product acceptance.
6. The report is critical and readable, not promotional, but it does not impersonate a named outlet.
7. Limitations and unresolved status are visible in both the report package and fact ledger.
