Notes · read after looking

Strike · Awake direction A

Spark
A struck spark: one oblique stroke leaving the lower left, three short rays flying off its tip up and to the right. The gap between stroke and rays is the moment of ignition; the weight sits low-left so the motion reads as departing, not as a star.
Attention
An eye that has just opened: only the upper lid and the pupil, no almond, no lashes. Without the lower lid it is not a watching eye but an awake one — the pupil rests on the open line of the lid.
Together
Both are one long line plus small marks. Spark leans and leaves; Attention is level and still. Same weight, opposite temperaments.

Pop · Hold direction B

Spark
A small thing bursting: a dot with three detached motion dashes up and to the right. The dashes never touch the dot, so it reads as a pop or a ping rather than a branch. The object is the dot; the change is happening around it.
Attention
A dot held in an open cup. The cup's rim is open to the top; nothing closes over the dot. Presence as being held and in view, not being tracked.
Together
The dot is the shared subject: in Spark it is bursting, in Attention it is held. Round, warm, and the most compact silhouettes of the three at 16px.

Sprout · Regard direction C

Spark
A shoot with a single leaf and a free grain of light above and to the left. Germination — the small change that is about to happen — with the loose dot as the lightness the leaf does not yet have.
Attention
A still centre between two open arcs — ( • ) — the look of something regarded from both sides without being enclosed. No ring closes, no lens, no target cross.
Together
Organic against geometric: the shoot grows, the arcs stay. The pair is the most poetic and the least like the neighbouring Lucide seats.

Recommendation

Direction A (Strike · Awake) is integrated. It is the only pair where both glyphs keep a clear silhouette at 16px in the real nav: the struck spark has a low-left weight and a direction, so it reads as something leaving rather than a star or a branch; the opened eye is level and calm, and because it has no lower lid it reads as awake rather than watching. B is the friendliest but the cup-and-dot reads as a face beside Chat, and the pop's dashes lose their gap at 16px. C is the most poetic and the least compatible: the shoot reads as a letter P at 16px, and ( • ) reads as sound or vibration. Trade-off accepted with A: the spark's three rays are a familiar comic device; the eye may read as a sunrise to some, which is within the 'awake' feel and not a surveillance reading.
