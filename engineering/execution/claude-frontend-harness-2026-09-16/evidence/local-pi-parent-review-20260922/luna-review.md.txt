## LP-R5 — Session deletion discards an unknown local-process fence

Severity: release blocker for the Host delete boundary.

The parent’s independent public DELETE probe is retained at /tmp/cw-local-pi-delete-probe-20260922.log. It reports:

    before {"runStatus":"unknown","fenced":true}
    after {"delete":{"status":200,...},"runPresent":false,"fenced":false,"assignmentStillPresent":true,"validationError":"Execution binding mismatch","providerRequests":0}

This was not rerun in this review, per the parent instruction.

Source cause:

- app/server/service.mjs:2253-2263 permits Session deletion when there is no active Run.
- app/server/store.mjs:1464-1474 removes the Session, its Runs, events and questions, but does not remove or settle the Subagents assignment/attempt that points to the deleted Run.
- app/runtime/local-pi-state.mjs:38-46 returns early when no local events or local Runs remain, so the local-process fence no longer validates.
- The retained assignment still references the deleted Run, and full validation reports Execution binding mismatch.

This violates the durable unknown rule: deleting a Session can erase the Run/event evidence while leaving the child attempt, allowing the unresolved local process identity to lose its fence. The final tree is therefore held for LP-R5. The correction belongs at the existing Host/Store deletion boundary: reject deletion for an unresolved local attempt, or preserve a durable tombstone/settled fence with the attempt. Do not silently delete the attempt or rely on the local validator after its Run is gone.
